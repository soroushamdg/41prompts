I've been building 41prompts, and this weekend I'm opening it up ahead of the Product Hunt launch on Monday.

If you work with LLMs, you probably have a prompt like this. It started as five lines and it's now forty, and every rule in it was added to fix something. Then a reply goes wrong, and nobody can say which line caused it or where the version that worked went.

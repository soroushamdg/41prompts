Neobrutalism × 6

Same components in every panel; only the tokens change. The variants differ on the axes that matter for a data-dense workbench, not on palette alone. Pass and fail contrast is checked in each.

border 1.5–3px shadow 3–5px shadow colour: black / accent / none radius 0–14px decorative saturation: max → zero type: display / grotesk / serif / mono

How to choose

  • The axis that matters is decorative saturation, not palette. Every colour you spend on decoration is one you cannot spend on pass/fail.
  • V2 keeps the construction and gives up the party. Black ink, hard shadows, zero decorative colour; the only saturated things on screen are states. It is the only variant where a red row is the loudest object.
  • V1 is the honest version of what you asked for and the one that will be hardest to read at 40 rows.
  • V4 is the one your users already live in. Terminal palette, mono everywhere, lime accent as the shadow colour. Strongest fit for the eval product, weakest for the lesson product aimed at newcomers.
  • V5 is the lesson-mode candidate. Same construction, softened; a beginner will not feel shouted at.
  • Running two variants is defensible: V4 or V2 for the workbench, V5 for lessons, V1 for the marketing site. Running three visual systems is not.
